#Anjo da Guarda

Anjo da Guarda is a mobile safety application designed to assist users in emergency situations by providing quick access to essential services. Whether you're in distress, need to illuminate a dark path, or share your current location with someone, Anjo da Guarda is there to help.

🚨 Key Features
Quick Access to Emergency Numbers
Instantly call Brazil’s major emergency services, including:

Police: 190

Fire Department: 193

Ambulance: 192

And more.

Flashlight Tool
Turn your phone into a flashlight with a single tap—ideal for power outages, night-time emergencies, or signaling for help.

Reverse Geolocation
Automatically detects and displays your current location as an address using GPS.

Location Sharing
Share your current location with trusted contacts or emergency responders quickly and easily.

More Safety Tools Coming Soon
We’re working on additional features to make Anjo da Guarda even more powerful and reliable.
